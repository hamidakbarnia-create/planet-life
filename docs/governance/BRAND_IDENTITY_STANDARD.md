# METIORO Brand Identity Standard

> Status: Draft

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Authority:** This document derives from [METIORO_CONSTITUTION.md](./METIORO_CONSTITUTION.md) (Level 1).

**Hierarchy level:** 1 — Brand Identity Standard

## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Scope

This Brand Identity Standard defines the permanent identity, expression, and boundaries of the METIORO brand. It governs how METIORO presents itself across all touchpoints — product copy, marketing, support communications, and governance documents — at the level of essence, promise, personality, voice, and visual principles.

This document does not define implementation. Colors, typography, spacing, UI components, and design tokens belong to the Design System and UX Standards (Level 4).

### 1.2 Relationship to the Constitution

This standard is subordinate to the METIORO Constitution. Where this document and the Constitution diverge, the Constitution prevails. All brand expression must remain compatible with:

- The product category: **Personal Decision Intelligence** (DEC-0001)
- The constitutional sentence: **"Know your best next move."**
- The locked brand tone pillars: **Calm**, **Scientific**, **Explainable**, **Never Mystical** (DEC-0004)
- The permanent principles in Constitution Chapter 3

No brand decision may contradict constitutional identity, boundaries, or non-negotiable principles.

## 2. Brand Essence

Brand essence is the irreducible character of METIORO — what the brand fundamentally is and how it orients toward the user.

| Essence | Definition |
|---------|------------|
| **Personal Decision Intelligence** | METIORO exists to support individual decision-making through structured, analytical guidance — not passive consumption or entertainment. |
| **Explainable** | Every brand expression must reflect that reasoning is visible and claims are traceable. Opaque mystique is incompatible with the brand. |
| **Calm** | The brand communicates with steadiness and composure. It does not alarm, rush, or sensationalize. |
| **Scientific** | The brand presents itself as method-aware, intellectually honest, and grounded in defensible analytical practice. |
| **Human-centered** | The brand serves the decision-maker. User agency, context, and judgment are respected at all times. |
| **Never Mystical** | The brand rejects esoteric, supernatural, occult, or fate-based framing. This is a permanent exclusion, not a stylistic preference. |

---

## 3. Brand Promise

### 3.1 Constitutional Sentence

The governing brand promise is:

> **Know your best next move.**

This sentence expresses orientation toward actionable clarity. It does not promise certainty, predetermined outcomes, or infallible prediction.

### 3.2 Guidance, Not Prediction

METIORO offers guidance based on structured analysis. The brand must never imply that METIORO knows what will happen, that outcomes are fated, or that recommendations guarantee success. METIORO **recommends**; the **user decides**.

### 3.3 Confidence Through Understanding

The brand builds confidence by helping users understand *why* a recommendation exists — not by asserting authority, certainty, or hidden knowledge. Trust is earned through transparency, not through claims of omniscience.

## 4. Personality

### 4.1 Permanent Traits

METIORO's brand personality is defined by the following permanent traits:

| Trait | Expression |
|-------|------------|
| **Calm** | Steady, measured, non-reactive. Does not create urgency where none exists. |
| **Clear** | Direct, unambiguous, free of jargon unless explained. |
| **Intelligent** | Thoughtful, analytical, capable of nuance without condescension. |
| **Honest** | Acknowledges limits, uncertainty, and the boundaries of its methods. |
| **Respectful** | Treats the user as a capable decision-maker, not a follower or believer. |
| **Curious** | Open to context and questions; invites understanding rather than demanding acceptance. |
| **Transparent** | Makes reasoning accessible; does not hide behind vague or oracular language. |

### 4.2 Prohibited Traits

METIORO must **never** adopt the following personality characteristics in any brand expression:

| Prohibited Trait | Reason |
|------------------|--------|
| **Mystical** | Violates constitutional identity and brand tone (DEC-0004). |
| **Fear-based** | Undermines user agency and trust; incompatible with Calm. |
| **Manipulative** | Violates human-centered principles and the recommendation model. |
| **Overconfident** | Violates Explainability Before Accuracy Claims (Constitution §3.2). |
| **Sensational** | Incompatible with Calm and Scientific tone. |
| **Dogmatic** | METIORO recommends; it does not decree. Users retain judgment. |

## 5. Voice & Tone

### 5.1 Writing Style

METIORO writes in a clear, analytical, and accessible style. Sentences are purposeful. Claims are supported. The voice is that of a knowledgeable advisor — not an oracle, entertainer, or authority figure.

### 5.2 Sentence Structure

- Prefer short to medium sentences.
- Lead with the actionable or informative point.
- Use active voice where possible.
- Break complex reasoning into sequential, readable steps.
- Avoid rhetorical questions designed to create mystery or suspense.

### 5.3 Reading Level

Communication should be intelligible to a general adult audience without requiring specialist knowledge. Technical terms may be used when necessary, but must be explained or contextualized on first use.

### 5.4 Vocabulary

- Use precise, plain language.
- Prefer analytical terms over emotional or esoteric ones.
- Avoid astrological jargon presented without explanation.
- Never use vocabulary associated with fortune telling, prophecy, divination, or mysticism (e.g. *destined*, *fated*, *cosmic sign*, *the universe wants*, *hidden forces*).

### 5.5 Emotional Tone

The default emotional tone is **calm**, **assured without arrogance**, and **supportive without pressure**. The brand may acknowledge difficulty or uncertainty but must not amplify anxiety to drive engagement.

### 5.6 Explicit Rules

1. **Never exaggerate certainty.** Do not use absolute language (*guaranteed*, *certain*, *will happen*, *definitely*) unless describing a factual, verifiable condition.
2. **Never use mystical language.** No supernatural, occult, or fate-based framing in any user-facing or marketing copy.
3. **Never pressure users.** Do not create artificial urgency, imply harm from non-compliance, or use fear to drive action.
4. **Always acknowledge uncertainty where appropriate.** When analytical limits exist, state them plainly.

## 6. Visual Identity Principles

Visual identity follows high-level principles only. Specific implementation — colors, typography, spacing, components, and layout tokens — belongs to the Design System and is not defined here.

| Principle | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Simplicity** | Visual expression should be clean and uncluttered. Every element must serve communication, not decoration. |
| **Precision** | Visual hierarchy should reflect analytical clarity. Information is structured, not ornamental. |
| **Calm visual hierarchy** | Layout and emphasis should guide the eye without agitation. Avoid visual noise, flashing urgency, or sensational contrast for engagement. |
| **Data-first communication** | Scores, reasoning, and analytical outputs take visual priority over decorative or emotional elements. |
| **Accessibility** | Visual identity must support readability, contrast, and inclusive access. Accessibility is a brand requirement, not an optional enhancement. |

## 7. Logo Governance

### 7.1 Locked Direction

The approved logo direction is **Concept B** (DEC-0003). This decision is locked and binding. The logo must communicate precision and intelligence without mystical or occult associations.

### 7.2 Implementation

Logo artwork, file formats, sizing rules, clear-space requirements, and usage variants are defined in the Design System — not in this document. This standard governs the *direction* and *governance* of the logo; implementation details belong elsewhere.

### 7.3 Asset Reference

The master logo asset is maintained at `brand/metioro-master.svg`. Modifications to the logo require compatibility with this standard and approval under the governance process defined in Chapter 9.

## 8. Brand Boundaries

### 8.1 METIORO Is Not

The brand must never be positioned as, or associated with:

- Fortune telling
- Psychic service
- Entertainment astrology
- Mysticism, occultism, or supernatural guidance

These exclusions are constitutional (Constitution §2.2) and permanent.

### 8.2 METIORO Is

The brand is constitutionally defined as:

| Identity | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Decision intelligence** | Structured analytical support for personal decision-making. |
| **Explainable guidance** | Recommendations accompanied by visible reasoning. |
| **Transparent reasoning** | Methods and limits are accessible, not concealed. |

---

## 9. Governance

### 9.1 Subordination to the Constitution

This Brand Identity Standard is a Level 1 document. It elaborates on constitutional brand commitments but may not override them. All brand decisions must be checked for constitutional compatibility before adoption.

### 9.2 Permanent Brand Changes

Changes to brand essence, promise, personality traits, prohibited traits, or brand boundaries are permanent decisions. They require:

1. Compatibility review against the Constitution.
2. Registration in [DECISION_LOG.md](./DECISION_LOG.md).
3. Approval by the project owner.
4. Constitutional amendment if the change affects non-negotiable principles or constitutional identity.

Cosmetic or implementation-level changes (e.g. color tokens, component styling) do not require constitutional amendment but must remain compatible with this standard.

### 9.3 Implementation Ownership

| Domain | Owner document / system |
|--------|-------------------------|
| Logo artwork and usage specs | Design System |
| UI components and layout | UX Standards (Level 4) |
| Product copy conventions | UX Standards / content guidelines |
| Marketing materials | Must conform to this standard |
| Legal disclaimers | Legal & Compliance Policies (Level 2) |

This standard defines *what* the brand is. Other documents and systems define *how* it is implemented.
